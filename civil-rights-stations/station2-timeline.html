Part A: Build the route

  1. Tap an event card on the right, then tap an empty stop on the route. (On a laptop you can also drag.)
  2. Put all 11 events in time order, earliest at the top. To move a card, tap it again.
  3. Press Check. Cards in the right place lock. The others go back so you can try again.

Part B: Match the songs

  1. Read each song ticket. The description is from your playlist assignment sheet.
  2. Tap a ticket, then tap the event it connects to most closely. Look for dates and event names.
  3. If it’s right, the song sticks to that stop. If not, read again and try another stop.
  4. When all 10 songs are placed, complete packet page 2.
