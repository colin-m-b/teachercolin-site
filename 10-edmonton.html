Unit 2 — What is History?

We study how historians turn evidence into explanations of the past, and why those explanations change over time, starting with the debate over who caused World War I.

Purpose for the unit

History is not just a list of facts to memorize. It is an argument about evidence, and that argument changes over time. In this unit students learn to think like historians: questioning sources, comparing perspectives, and asking who gets to tell the story and what has been left out. We put these skills to work on a real historical debate: who was responsible for World War I? These are the same skills students need to judge news, claims, and arguments in a world full of competing information.

Key concept: Change · Global context: Personal and cultural expression
