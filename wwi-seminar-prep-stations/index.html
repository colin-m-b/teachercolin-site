Seminar Prep Stations

Four stations on how historians argue about the causes of the First World War. Each one prepares you for two of the seminar’s focus questions.

15 minutes eachYour group rotates when Mr. Colin calls time.
One worksheetWrite every answer on your seminar prep worksheet.
Bring your packetsThe stations use your primary and secondary source packets.
1

Fact or Interpretation?

What is the difference between a fact about the past and an interpretation of it?

Prepares you for Q1 and Q7
2

Why Do Historians Disagree?

Historians study the same events. Why do they reach different conclusions?

Prepares you for Q3 and Q5
3

Who Writes History?

Is history written by the winners? How much does a historian’s own time and place shape their view?

Prepares you for Q2 and Q6
4

What If?

Can imagining a different past help us understand what really caused the war?

Prepares you for Q4 and Q8
