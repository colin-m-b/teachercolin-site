Why Do Historians Disagree?

Historians study the same events. Why do they reach different conclusions?

This station prepares you for

Q3. Why do historians disagree about what country was responsible for starting WW1?

Q5. What are the causes that lead to misalignment or historical interpretations by different historians?

Concept to use: perspective. Why does the same evidence lead different historians to different conclusions?

Three reasons historians disagree

DE
Different evidence
Some sources become available later, or one historian uses sources another did not.
TE
Trusting evidence differently
Historians read the same source but decide differently how reliable or important it is.
DV
Different values
When and where a historian lives shapes what they think matters and how they judge people.
