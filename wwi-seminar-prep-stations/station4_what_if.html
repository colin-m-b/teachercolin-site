What If?

Can imagining a different past help us understand what really caused the war?

This station prepares you for

Q4. A historical counterfactual is imagining what would the result have been if an aspect of history was different. For example, “What would happen if Russia did not order general mobilisation or the information was not leaked? How would other countries respond to this?” Do counterfactuals help us understand history? Why or why not?

Q8. Could WWI be avoided if not for the assasination of the duke, if then what could have been the alternate cause for WWI?
